About · Lake Country, Fort Worth

Family owned since 2014.

Dr. Royce Reese opened Bliss Modern Dentistry in 2014. Since his retirement in 2022 the practice has stayed family owned, with the same promise: treat every patient like family.

Our practice

Modern, and personal.

Our friendly, experienced team will welcome you warmly and make sure you feel comfortable throughout your visit. We put patient care first, use modern dental technology, and take time to explain your options so you can make informed decisions.

  • A calm, comfortable office with modern amenities
  • Digital X-rays and 3D scanning
  • Patients regularly mention short waits and on-time appointments
  • Scholarships each year for seniors from Lake Worth High School and the Eagle Mountain-Saginaw district
