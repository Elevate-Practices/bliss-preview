Welcome to Bliss.

Your first visit

What to expect.

  1. 1

    Book your visit

    Online or by phone. Let us know if you're nervous or have a specific concern.

  2. 2

    Health history

    We review your medical history and the medications you take, since some can affect your dental care.

  3. 3

    Complete exam

    A full-mouth series of X-rays and a thorough exam of your teeth, gums, mouth and jaw, with a non-invasive oral cancer screening.

  4. 4

    Your plan, explained

    We walk you through what we found and your options, so you can make informed decisions.

New patients

New patient questions.

Do I need to fill out forms before my visit?

You don't have to, but it saves time. Download the new patient forms, print them and bring them to your first appointment. Please don't email completed forms.

What should I bring?

Your completed forms if you printed them, a list of your medications and dosages (including vitamins and supplements), your dental insurance card if you have one, and your questions.

Do you take my insurance?

We work with most PPO plans and file your claims for you as a courtesy. Call (817) 500-5288 and we'll check your plan.

What if I'm nervous?

Tell us when you book. We'll go at your pace and explain each step before we do it.
