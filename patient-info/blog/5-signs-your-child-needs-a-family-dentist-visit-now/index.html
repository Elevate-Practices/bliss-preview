5 Signs Your Child Needs a Family Dentist Visit Now

Kids can't always tell you what's wrong, and small dental problems grow quickly. If you notice any of these five signs, it's time to book a visit at our Lake Country office in Fort Worth.

1. Toothache or dental pain

Tooth pain in children can point to a cavity, an infection or a tooth coming in. If your child complains of discomfort that doesn't go away, don't wait. An exam and X-rays can find the cause and bring relief before a small problem turns into a bigger one.

2. Bleeding or sore gums

Healthy gums don't bleed. If your child's gums are red, swollen or bleed when they brush, it can mean plaque buildup, gum irritation or brushing technique that needs help. The good news is that early gum problems are usually easy to turn around.

3. Bad breath that won't go away

Occasional bad breath is normal. Breath that lingers after brushing can signal cavities, gum problems or something else worth checking, so it's worth having a dentist take a look.

4. Loose or misaligned teeth

Losing baby teeth is normal at certain ages. But teeth that loosen too early, come in crowded or look crooked deserve an exam so your child's bite and jaw develop on track.

5. Trouble eating or chewing

If your child avoids certain foods, chews on one side or complains while eating, the cause may be a sore tooth or a bite problem. Mealtime should be easy and pain-free.

Why regular checkups matter for kids

Most dental problems in children are preventable. Regular cleanings and exams keep teeth clean, catch trouble early and help kids feel comfortable at the dentist. Our Child membership is $39 a month for ages 12 and younger and includes cleanings, exams, fluoride and needed X-rays.

Don't wait for pain. Book your child's visit or call us, and we'll find a time that works for your family.

FAQ

Quick answers.

When should my child first see a dentist?

By their first birthday or when the first tooth comes in, then on the schedule their dental team recommends, usually every six months.

Is it normal for kids to be nervous at the dentist?

Yes. Tell us when you book, and we'll go slowly, explain each step in simple words and give your child time to get comfortable.

My child has a toothache right now. What should I do?

Call us. We offer same-day emergency visits, and we can tell you what to do until you arrive.
