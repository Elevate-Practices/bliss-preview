What to Expect at Your First Dental Visit in Fort Worth

Starting with a new dentist can feel like a big step, especially if it has been a while since your last visit. The good news: a first visit at a good dental office is mostly a conversation. Here is what to expect at Bliss Modern Dentistry in Lake Country, Fort Worth.

Before you arrive

Book online or call us at the front desk, and tell us anything that would help us plan, including if dental visits make you nervous. We'll send you our new patient forms so you can fill them out at home, which makes check-in quick. Bring a photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, and a list of your medications.

Your first visit, step by step

  1. We get to know you. We review your health history and medications and listen to your goals, concerns and past experiences.
  2. A complete exam. This includes digital X-rays, a full exam of your teeth, gums and jaw, and an oral cancer screening.
  3. A cleaning, when it fits. Depending on what we find, we may be able to clean your teeth the same day. If your gums need extra attention, we'll explain why and what comes next.
  4. Your plan, explained. We show you what we found on the screen and walk through your options in plain words, with no surprises on cost.

What if I'm nervous?

You are not alone, and you won't be judged. Tell us when you book and again when you sit down. We can go slowly, explain each step before we do it, and pause whenever you need a break.

How much will it cost?

We'll talk about cost before any treatment starts. We file most PPO plans for you. If you don't have insurance, our in-house membership starts at $39 a month and covers your cleanings, exams, fluoride and needed X-rays.

Ready when you are. Book your first visit or call us, and we'll find a time that works for you.

FAQ

Quick answers.

How long is a first dental visit?

Plan for a little longer than a regular cleaning, since we review your history, take X-rays and do a full exam. Ask the front desk when you book and they'll give you a good estimate.

Do I need to bring my old dental records?

It helps, but it is not required. If you have recent X-rays at another office, tell us and we can request them.

What if I haven't been to the dentist in years?

That's very common, and we'll treat you with kindness, not judgment. We'll start with an exam, explain what we find, and build a plan at a pace you're comfortable with.

Questions about your own smile?

Our team is happy to help.
