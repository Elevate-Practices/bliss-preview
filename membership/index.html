Membership

Membership, answered.

Is the membership plan dental insurance?

No. It's an in-house membership: you pay a monthly fee for your preventive care and get a discount on other treatment. There are no claim forms, deductibles, annual maximums or pre-approvals.

When can I start using it?

Right away. Your membership starts as soon as you enroll, with no waiting period.

Which plan is right for me?

The Adult plan is for patients 13 and older with regular cleanings and no gum disease. The Perio plan is for patients in periodontal maintenance after gum disease treatment. The Child plan is for kids 12 and younger.

What is the activation fee?

Each plan has a one-time $99 lifetime activation fee, which includes your first month.

What does the 15% discount cover?

Members save 15% on all other treatments, with some exclusions. Payment for cosmetic or restorative treatment is due at the time of service.
