FAQ

Questions, answered.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. Kids, teens and adults are all welcome. Book online or call (817) 500-5288.

Do you take my insurance?

We work with most PPO dental insurance plans and file your claims for you. Call (817) 500-5288 and we'll check your plan.

What if I don't have insurance?

Our membership plan starts at $39 a month for kids and $49 for adults. It covers cleanings, exams, fluoride and needed X-rays, plus 15% off other treatment. See the plans.

Do you see emergencies?

Yes, with same-day visits when the schedule allows. If you have tooth pain, swelling or a broken tooth, call (817) 500-5288 as early in the day as you can.

What are your hours?

Monday to Thursday, 8 AM to 5 PM, and Friday, 8 AM to 1 PM.

Where are you?

8525 Boat Club Road in the Lake Country neighborhood of northwest Fort Worth, near the Fort Worth Nature Center & Refuge. Patients also come from Lake Worth, Lakeside, Azle, Saginaw, Eagle Mountain, Reno and Springtown.
