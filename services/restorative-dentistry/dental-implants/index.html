What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone to act as artificial tooth roots. Once healed, these posts support custom crowns, bridges, or dentures. Implants are uniquely designed to fuse with the bone, offering unmatched stability and longevity.

Unlike traditional bridges or removable dentures, implants:

  • Prevent bone loss in the jaw
  • Restore full biting strength
  • Do not affect neighboring healthy teeth
  • Can last a lifetime with proper care

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Dental implants are a great choice for most adults with missing teeth. However, ideal candidates typically:

  • Have one or more missing teeth
  • Possess adequate bone density in the jaw
  • Do not smoke or are willing to quit during healing
  • Are in overall good oral and physical health

If you have bone loss or have been missing teeth for a long time, we may recommend preparatory procedures like bone grafting.

The Dental Implant Process at Bliss Modern Dentistry

At Bliss Modern Dentistry, our clinical team provide personalized implant care from start to finish. Your treatment journey typically includes:

  1. Consultation and Imaging – We begin with a thorough evaluation and 3D imaging to determine suitability.
  2. Surgical Placement – The implant is placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia.
  3. Healing Period – Over several months, the implant fuses with your bone (osseointegration).
  4. Restoration Placement – Once healed, we secure a custom dental crown, bridge, or denture to complete your smile.

Throughout every phase, our team prioritizes comfort, communication, and precision. Our modern technology and patient-centered approach help ensure optimal outcomes.

Dental Implants

Dental Implants, answered.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last several decades—often a lifetime. Regular cleanings and checkups help maintain the health of the implant and surrounding gum tissue.

Does getting a dental implant hurt?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia and is often less uncomfortable than patients expect. Mild soreness after surgery is normal and manageable with over-the-counter medication.

What if I’m missing multiple teeth?

Implants can replace single teeth or support full arch prosthetics. Implant-supported bridges and dentures are excellent solutions for multiple missing teeth.

Are dental implants covered by insurance?

Coverage varies depending on the provider and plan. We work closely with patients to maximize their insurance benefits and offer financing through third-party providers when needed.

What’s the difference between implants and dentures?

Dentures rest on the gums and can shift over time. Implants, on the other hand, are anchored directly into the bone, providing superior stability and function.
