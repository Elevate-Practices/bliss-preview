Dental restorations are treatments designed to repair and restore damaged or lost teeth to their optimal form and function. These procedures are crucial for maintaining oral health, preventing further decay, and enhancing the aesthetics of your smile, giving patients benefits including:

  • Restored Functionality: Restorations like fillings, crowns, and bridges are designed to restore the function of damaged or missing teeth, enabling patients to chew and speak properly.
  • Improved Oral Health: By addressing tooth decay, damage, and loss, restorative procedures prevent further oral health complications, including infection and jawbone deterioration.
  • Enhanced Aesthetics: Modern dental materials used in restorations mimic the appearance of natural teeth, improving the overall look of the smile and boosting self-confidence.
  • Prevention of Future Issues: Restorative treatments help maintain the alignment of teeth by preventing the shifting of neighboring teeth into gaps, reducing the risk of bite problems and jaw pain.
  • Longevity and Durability: With advancements in dental technology, restorative solutions like implants and crowns are designed to last many years, providing a long-term solution to dental issues.

The journey to achieving a restored and healthy smile at Bliss Modern Dentistry involves several steps, ensuring personalized and effective treatment:

  1. Initial Consultation and Assessment: The process begins with a thorough examination of the patient’s oral health, including the use of digital imaging to assess the extent of damage and plan the restoration accurately.
  2. Treatment Planning: Our team discuss the most suitable restorative options with the patient, considering factors like the patient’s health, aesthetic goals, and budget.
  3. Preparation of the Tooth/Teeth: For crowns, bridges, and fillings, the affected tooth may need to be prepared by removing decay or reshaping. For implants, this step involves preparing the jawbone for implant placement.
  4. Creating and Placing the Restoration: Restorations like crowns and bridges are custom-made in a dental lab to match the patient’s natural teeth in color and shape. Dental implants involve a surgical procedure to place the implant post in the jawbone, followed by a healing period before attaching the crown or bridge.
  5. Follow-Up and Aftercare: After the restoration is placed, patients will have follow-up visits to ensure the fit and function are perfect and to make any necessary adjustments. Patients are also educated on how to care for their restorations to ensure their longevity.

Advanced Restorative Technology

We utilize state-of-the-art technology, such as digital imaging and CAD/CAM systems, to ensure precise and efficient restorations. These advanced tools help in creating restorations that are not only effective but also aesthetically pleasing and long-lasting.

Customized Restoration Plans

Each patient’s dental needs are unique, and we provide customized restoration plans tailored to individual requirements. Our approach ensures that you receive the most appropriate and effective treatments for your specific dental issues.

Daily Care and Hygiene

  • Regular Brushing and Flossing: Just like natural teeth, restored teeth require regular brushing and flossing to prevent plaque buildup and decay. Patients should brush at least twice a day and floss daily to maintain the health of the restoration and surrounding gum tissue.
  • Use of Non-Abrasive Toothpaste: It’s advisable to use a non-abrasive toothpaste, especially for restorations like crowns and veneers, to prevent scratching or damaging the surface.

Regular Dental Check-ups and Cleanings

  • Routine Examinations: Regular check-ups at Bliss Modern Dentistry allow for the monitoring of restorations and the early detection of any issues that may require attention, ensuring the longevity of the dental work.
  • Professional Cleanings: Scheduled cleanings help remove tartar and plaque that can't be eliminated by brushing and flossing alone, protecting both natural teeth and restorative work.

Protecting Your Restorations

  • Avoiding Hard and Sticky Foods: To prevent damage to restorations, patients should avoid chewing hard objects (like ice or hard candy) and be cautious with sticky foods that can dislodge or damage restorative work.
  • Wearing Mouth Guards: For those who grind their teeth at night or participate in contact sports, a custom-fitted mouth guard can protect restorations from undue stress and potential damage.

Addressing Habits and Conditions

  • Managing Bruxism: Patients with bruxism (teeth grinding) may need special care or adjustments to their restorations and possibly a night guard to protect against grinding damage.
  • Controlling Gum Disease: Maintaining healthy gums is essential for the success of restorations, particularly for implants and bridgework. Regular hygiene appointments and following prescribed periodontal care routines can prevent gum disease that might compromise restorative work.

Follow-Up Care

After a restoration is placed, the team at Bliss Modern Dentistry will provide specific care instructions and schedule follow-up appointments to ensure the restoration is functioning properly and comfortably.
