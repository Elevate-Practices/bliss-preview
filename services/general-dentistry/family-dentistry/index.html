Family Dentistry in Fort Worth, TX

Bliss Modern Dentistry is proud to be a trusted provider of family dentistry in Fort Worth, TX. From toddlers to seniors, our practice delivers personalized care for patients of all ages, ensuring that every member of your family receives high-quality dental treatment in one convenient location.

Comprehensive Care for Every Stage of Life

Situated in the Lake Country neighborhood, our office serves families from Lakeside, Lake Worth, and throughout the greater Fort Worth area. Whether it's your child's first checkup or you're managing multiple oral health needs, our team is here to guide your family toward lasting dental wellness.

What Is Family Dentistry?

Family dentistry focuses on delivering preventive, diagnostic, and restorative dental services for all age groups. At Bliss Modern Dentistry, we combine clinical expertise with a warm, welcoming atmosphere—making dental visits comfortable and productive for everyone in the household.

Our family dentistry services include:

  • Routine cleanings and exams
  • Pediatric checkups and sealants
  • Fluoride treatments
  • Dental fillings and crowns
  • Extractions when necessary
  • Education on home care habits

We emphasize preventative care as the foundation of lifelong oral health, while also offering treatments tailored to the evolving needs of growing children, busy parents, and aging adults.

A Family-Friendly Experience from Start to Finish

Our practice was founded in 2014 and we continue the tradition of patient-centered care originally established by Dr. Royce Reese. Patients consistently mention the approachable, calming presence of our providers and staff—especially helpful for families with young children or members who experience dental anxiety.

Families choose Bliss Modern Dentistry because we provide:

  • A compassionate, judgment-free environment
  • Streamlined appointment scheduling for multiple family members
  • Advanced digital technology for accurate diagnostics
  • Continuity of care under one roof
  • A location convenient to Fort Worth neighborhoods including Lakeside and Lake Worth

Parents appreciate the trust and transparency we offer, and children enjoy the relaxed atmosphere and friendly staff. Our hygienist, Linda, is especially known for making young patients feel at ease during their cleanings.

Full-Spectrum Dental Services for the Whole Family

In addition to family dentistry, we provide a wide range of services so your family can stay with a single trusted team over time. These include:

  • General Dentistry: Cleanings, exams, extractions, and more
  • Cosmetic Dentistry: Options to brighten, straighten, or enhance your smile
  • Invisalign: Clear aligners for teens and adults
  • Restorative Dentistry: Including crowns, dentures, and implants
  • Emergency Services: Same-day care for urgent dental needs

By offering comprehensive services under one roof, we make it easier for your family to stay on track with their oral health—no need for referrals or juggling multiple offices.

Family Dentistry

Family Dentistry, answered.

At what age should my child first visit the dentist?

We recommend scheduling your child’s first visit around their first birthday or within six months of their first tooth appearing. Early visits establish good habits and allow us to monitor healthy development.

Do you offer fluoride treatments and sealants for kids?

Yes. We often recommend fluoride and dental sealants as effective, non-invasive ways to protect children’s teeth from cavities.

Can you accommodate back-to-back appointments for families?

Absolutely. We offer coordinated scheduling so that parents and children can receive care together or in close succession, saving you time and hassle.

What if my child is nervous about dental visits?

We’re experienced in working with children who have dental anxiety. Our friendly team and calm approach help young patients feel comfortable and safe in our care.

Are evening or weekend appointments available?

While hours were not provided, we invite you to contact our office directly to ask about flexible scheduling options for busy families.
