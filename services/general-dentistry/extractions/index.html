When Is a Tooth Extraction Needed?

Tooth extractions may be recommended when preserving the tooth is no longer the healthiest or safest option. During your evaluation, our dentists will carefully assess your condition and discuss all appropriate treatment options before recommending removal.

Common reasons for tooth extractions include:

  • Severe tooth decay
  • Cracked or fractured teeth below the gumline
  • Advanced periodontal disease
  • Impacted wisdom teeth
  • Dental infections
  • Orthodontic overcrowding
  • Teeth damaged by trauma or injury

Patients experiencing severe pain, swelling, or signs of infection may also benefit from prompt evaluation through our Same-Day Emergency Dental Care services.

Wisdom Teeth Extractions in Fort Worth, TX

Wisdom teeth commonly emerge during the late teens or early twenties and may create problems such as crowding, discomfort, swelling, or infection. In some cases, wisdom teeth may become impacted beneath the gums or grow at improper angles.

At Bliss Modern Dentistry, we perform wisdom teeth extractions using advanced digital imaging and personalized treatment planning to help ensure safe, effective care.

Wisdom teeth removal may be recommended if you experience:

  • Jaw pain or pressure
  • Swollen gums
  • Recurring infections
  • Difficulty cleaning wisdom teeth
  • Damage to neighboring teeth
  • Orthodontic crowding concerns

We offer local anesthesia and sedation options to help patients feel more relaxed and comfortable during treatment.

Patients from Fort Worth, Azle, Reno, Weatherford, and Springtown often choose our office for wisdom teeth evaluations because of our convenient location and compassionate approach to care.

What to Expect During a Tooth Extraction

Our goal is to make the extraction process as smooth and comfortable as possible. Every treatment plan is customized to the patient’s needs and oral health condition.

The extraction process generally includes:

  1. Comprehensive dental exam and digital X-rays
  2. Review of treatment recommendations
  3. Local anesthesia to numb the area
  4. Gentle tooth removal using precise techniques
  5. Post-operative instructions for healing and recovery

After your procedure, our team will provide guidance to help reduce discomfort and support proper healing. Most patients recover within approximately 7–10 days, though healing times can vary depending on the type of extraction performed.

Restoring Your Smile After Tooth Removal

Following a tooth extraction, restoring the missing tooth may help support chewing function, smile appearance, and long-term oral health. our dentists will discuss appropriate replacement options based on your needs and goals.

Restorative options may include:

  • Dental Implants
  • Dentures and Partials
  • Dental Crowns

As part of our comprehensive General Dentistry Services, we help patients make informed decisions about restoring oral health after tooth loss.
