Comfortable, Efficient Root Canal Therapy to Save Your Natural Tooth

Root canal treatment may sound intimidating, but it’s actually a routine, highly successful procedure that can relieve severe pain, stop infection, and prevent tooth loss. Most patients report that the experience is no more uncomfortable than getting a filling.

What Is a Root Canal?

A root canal becomes necessary when the pulp inside your tooth—the soft tissue containing nerves and blood vessels—becomes inflamed or infected due to deep decay, repeated dental procedures, cracks, or trauma. If left untreated, this infection can spread and may result in abscesses or even loss of the tooth.

Root canal therapy involves:

  1. Removing the infected or damaged pulp tissue
  2. Cleaning and disinfecting the canals
  3. Sealing the canals to prevent future infection
  4. Restoring the tooth with a crown for full function and protection

We often follow a root canal with a custom dental crown to reinforce the tooth’s structure and help it function like new.

Signs You Might Need Root Canal Treatment

Not every toothache means you need a root canal, but certain symptoms can indicate deeper issues:

  • Persistent toothache or sensitivity to hot and cold
  • Pain when biting or chewing
  • Darkening or discoloration of the tooth
  • Swollen gums around one tooth
  • Pimple-like bump on the gums (possible abscess)
  • Deep decay near the root

If you’re experiencing any of these symptoms, schedule a visit right away. We offer same-day emergency appointments for urgent care.

What to Expect at Bliss Modern Dentistry

From diagnosis to recovery, your comfort is our priority. When you visit Bliss Modern Dentistry for a root canal in Fort Worth, you can expect:

  • Digital X-rays and advanced imaging to assess the tooth
  • Local anesthetic to numb the area completely
  • Precise removal of infected tissue using modern rotary instruments
  • Thorough cleaning and sealing of the tooth's canals
  • Discussion of next steps, including crown restoration
  • Aftercare guidance for smooth recovery

We typically complete the procedure in one visit, though complex cases may require a follow-up. For your convenience, we also offer comprehensive general dentistry services in one location—so you don’t have to visit multiple offices for follow-up care.

Benefits of Root Canal Therapy

Choosing to treat the issue with a root canal instead of extracting the tooth offers several benefits:

  • Saves your natural tooth
  • Restores normal biting and chewing
  • Maintains proper alignment of surrounding teeth
  • Prevents bone loss in the jaw
  • Eliminates the need for more costly restorative options

If a tooth can be saved, root canal therapy is nearly always the preferred solution. For severely damaged teeth, we also offer restorative options like dental implants and dentures, depending on your long-term goals.

Root Canals

Root Canals, answered.

Is a root canal painful?

Thanks to local anesthesia and advanced techniques, most patients find the procedure to be no more uncomfortable than a typical dental filling. Discomfort after treatment is usually mild and manageable.

How long does a root canal take?

Most root canals can be completed in one appointment lasting between 60 and 90 minutes. Some teeth with more complex anatomy may require a second visit.

Will I need a crown after a root canal?

In most cases, yes. A dental crown helps protect the treated tooth from future fractures and restores full function.

Can I go to work or school after the procedure?

Many patients return to their routine activities the same day, especially if only local anesthetic was used. However, some prefer to rest for the remainder of the day.

How do I know if a root canal failed?

Signs of a failing root canal can include recurring pain, swelling, or tenderness near the treated tooth. If you notice any of these symptoms, contact us for an evaluation right away.
