Preventive Teeth Cleaning and Dental Exams for All Ages

Our team delivers personalized preventive care in a comfortable, family-friendly setting. Whether it’s your first visit or your next six-month checkup, we focus on keeping your teeth and gums clean, healthy, and strong.

What Happens During a Dental Cleaning?

A dental cleaning, also called a prophylaxis, involves the removal of plaque, tartar, and stains from the teeth. Even if you brush and floss daily, some buildup can only be removed with professional tools.

Here’s what to expect at your appointment:

  1. Plaque and Tartar Removal – Using specialized instruments, our hygienists remove hardened buildup around the teeth and along the gumline.
  2. Polishing – After scaling, your teeth are polished to remove surface stains and leave your smile feeling smooth and fresh.
  3. Fluoride (if recommended) – Fluoride treatment may be applied to help strengthen enamel and protect against cavities, especially for children or those prone to decay.
  4. Dental Exam – Our teamwill perform a thorough dental exam to check for signs of cavities, gum disease, bite issues, and oral cancer.

Our cleanings are designed to be gentle and efficient. We welcome patients of all ages and tailor our approach based on individual comfort levels and health needs.

Why Are Regular Teeth Cleanings So Important?

Professional teeth cleaning plays a crucial role in long-term oral health. Benefits include:

  • Removing plaque and tartar that brushing alone can't reach
  • Preventing gum disease and tooth decay
  • Detecting dental problems early through routine exams
  • Keeping breath fresh and teeth visibly cleaner
  • Supporting overall health—gum disease has been linked to systemic conditions like heart disease and diabetes

Most patients benefit from dental cleanings every six months, though some may require more frequent visits based on their oral health status.

Cleanings & Exams

Cleanings & Exams, answered.

How often should I get a dental cleaning?

Most patients should schedule cleanings every six months. If you have gum disease, diabetes, or other oral health concerns, our team may recommend more frequent visits.

Are dental cleanings painful?

Cleanings are generally painless. If you have sensitive gums or teeth, let us know in advance so we can adjust our technique to ensure your comfort.

What’s the difference between a dental cleaning and a deep cleaning?

A standard cleaning focuses on preventing disease. A deep cleaning, or scaling and root planing, is a more intensive procedure used to treat existing gum disease.

Will insurance cover my cleaning?

Most dental insurance plans cover two cleanings and exams per year. Our front desk team can help confirm your benefits and file claims on your behalf.

Can I get a cleaning if I’m pregnant?

Yes, dental cleanings and exams are safe during pregnancy and are encouraged to maintain oral health, especially since gum sensitivity can increase during this time.
