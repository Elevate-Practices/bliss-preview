Our Teeth Whitening Services in Fort Worth, TX

Bliss Modern Dentistry offers a suite of teeth whitening services designed to meet every need and lifestyle. Our in-office whitening procedure provides dramatic results in just one visit, using advanced light-activated technology for a brighter smile. For those who prefer the convenience of home treatment, we provide professional take-home kits, complete with custom-fitted trays and professional-grade whitening gel. Each option is crafted to deliver optimal results, ensuring your smile shines its brightest.

The Teeth Whitening Process: What to Expect

Embarking on your teeth whitening journey with Bliss Modern Dentistry begins with a comprehensive consultation. This initial step ensures your dental health is suitable for whitening and helps tailor the treatment to your specific goals.

For in-office whitening, expect a single session lasting about an hour. The process involves applying a high-concentration peroxide gel to the teeth, activated by a special light to accelerate the whitening effect.

Opting for a take-home kit? We'll first take impressions of your teeth to create custom trays, ensuring even gel coverage and comfort during treatment. We provide detailed instructions on how to use your kit at home for the best results.

Caring for Your Brighter Smile

Maintaining your newly whitened smile is straightforward with our expert advice. Avoid or limit foods and drinks that can stain your teeth, such as coffee, red wine, and dark berries. Regular brushing, flossing, and dental check-ups are key to prolonging the effects of teeth whitening. We may also recommend specific toothpaste or products to help maintain your results.

How Long Will My Whitening Results Last?

The longevity of teeth whitening results varies depending on lifestyle and oral hygiene. Generally, you can enjoy a brighter smile for one to three years. To maintain your results, we recommend avoiding stain-causing foods and beverages, along with regular cleanings and touch-up treatments at Bliss Modern Dentistry.

Is Teeth Whitening Safe?

Yes, teeth whitening performed by dental professionals is safe. At Bliss Modern Dentistry, we use advanced techniques and materials to ensure your treatment is effective without compromising your oral health. We assess your dental history and current oral health to determine the best whitening approach for you.

Will Teeth Whitening Cause Sensitivity?

Some patients may experience temporary sensitivity after teeth whitening. We use carefully controlled whitening agents and provide post-treatment care tips to minimize any discomfort. If sensitivity occurs, it typically subsides within a few days after the treatment.

Can Teeth Whitening Damage Enamel?

Our teeth whitening procedures are designed to be gentle yet effective, ensuring there is no damage to the enamel. We use clinically approved products that are tested for safety and efficacy, prioritizing the health of your teeth.
